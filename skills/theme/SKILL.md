# More Gravity Theme Studio

Opens the official Theme Studio auxiliary drawer on the right side of Antigravity 2.0 to customize dark mode themes and color accents.

## Auxiliary Panel Pill
Click this pill to toggle the right-hand Theme Studio drawer:

[🎨 Theme Studio](sidecar://more-gravity-theme/panel/)

## Available Calibrated Themes
1. **Everforest Dark** - Warm analog forest green (`#a7c080`)
2. **Catppuccin Mocha** - Soothing pastel peach (`#fab387`) & lavender
3. **Flexoki Dark** - Inky paper tone with mineral ochre (`#da702c`)
4. **Kanagawa Dragon** - Sumi-e dark peat with autumn foliage (`#b6927b`)
5. **Melange Dark** - Warm earth peat, stone, and vermilion (`#d47766`)
